# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want a community where people can learn digital forensics without
harassment. We pledge to make participation free from harassment for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Showing the evidence for a claim, including a citation or a failing
  test.
- Accepting correction.
- Focusing on what is best for the people who will rely on a triage
  draft.

Examples of behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, including real case evidence,
  without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will remove or edit comments, commits, code, and
issues that are not aligned with this code of conduct, and will ban a
contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, or
harmful.

Report conduct concerns through a private GitHub security advisory on
this repository. Do not file a public issue for a safety report, and
do not include real evidence.
